Maison Baer

Maison Baer is a private dinner club. One table, seven guests, never an eighth.

One long evening, a menu that is never served twice, and a conversation that decides for itself when it ends.

It began with a simple wish: to bring together people who would rather listen than impress.

Membership is offered on request, through those already at the table.
